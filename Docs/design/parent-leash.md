# Parent leash

## Note 46

Parent leash: Units stay near the grid that spawned them, and those Units never create KPLs (GVK-Settings#709; checked against MES `d500922`). Generated by `tools/New-LeashGroup.ps1`.
- **Parent side:** `GVK-Universal-TriggerGroup-LeashBeacon` on every behavior that spawns Units (Defense waves, air support, Elite escorts, Convoy leads, Freighters). Every 5 s it sends `GVK_LeashGround` (3 km) and `GVK_LeashAir` (5 km) with `[MatchReceiverChild:true]`, so only the sender's own children hear it. Each signal carries the parent's position (`GVK-Universal-LeashBeacon-Waypoint-Self`).
- **No owner check:** `[MatchSenderReceiverOwners:false]`, because the owner check is on by default and a parent's children are not always its own faction (a DERELICT wreck spawns GAALSIEN Defense Units, a GAALSIEN wreck GAALSIEN ones, a Freighter its own Defenses and enemy attackers). The child filter alone works for every pairing.
- **Unit side:** every Unit carries `GVK-Unit-All-TriggerGroup-LeashGround` or `-LeashAir`. Hearing the signal sets `GVK_HasParent`, which blocks `CreateKPL`, and moves the Unit's patrol centre to the parent (`[UseCommandWaypointAsPatrolReference:true]`). After 15 s without it, `GVK_AwayFromHome` blocks `GVK-Unit-All-Condition-CanStartCombat` and the GVK-TriggerTags-SwitchToPatrol tag sends the Unit back to Patrol, around where it last heard its parent. The next signal clears it.
- **Letting go:** a Compromised parent sends `GVK_LeashRelease`. A Unit that stays away for 3 more minutes lets go by itself, which covers a parent that despawned or drove out of range. A released Unit patrols around its spawn point again (`[ClearCustomPatrolReference:true]`), fights anywhere and still creates no KPL.
- **Moving parents:** the patrol centre can't follow a parent that moves. MES Patrol reads the centre only when a leg starts (at Init, or on reaching a patrol point). Its re-rolls (the `WaypointAbandonTimeTrigger` timer and the collision check) keep the old centre, so Units circle where the parent was and fall behind (GVK-Settings#764, MES `Patrol.cs` at `d500922`). They also patrol at 40 m/s, below a Freighter's 50-60 m/s. The real fix is an MES change; until then a moving parent uses escort slots.
- **Escort slots:** a moving parent also carries `GVK-Universal-TriggerGroup-LeashSlots` and `[EscortOffsets:]` lines (the Freighters, as of 2026-11-08). Every 5 s it offers a slot to its own children of its own faction within 8 km: `[MatchReceiverChild:true]` with the owner check on, so the enemy attackers it spawns never get one.
  - A `-LeashAir` Unit asks for the slot when it isn't a wing, isn't fighting and isn't waiting on another request (`GVK-Unit-All-Condition-CanTakeSlot`). It then flies in the slot as an Escort on its Tertiary wing autopilot, with `GVK_InParentSlot` and `GVK_Role_Escort` true. Ground Units take no slots.
  - It leaves the slot when its `Patrol<Name>` group starts combat (the GVK-TriggerTags-CombatStarted tag, at that group's own TargetNear distance), when the parent stops working (`BehaviorTriggerB`) or on `GVK_LeashRelease`. It goes back to Patrol, and the GVK-TriggerTags-LeaderDead tag gives it its combat switch at once. When its group puts it back on Patrol, it asks again.
  - The parent answers every request, even when it's full, because MES sends the reply before it assigns the slot. A Unit whose Escort behavior finds no slot within 5 s (no `BehaviorTriggerA`) goes back to Patrol and stops asking for 60 s. That is well before MES's one-way Escort Retreat (30 s on `GVK-Unit-All-Autopilot-AirWing`).
  - A peer leader can take a slot; its wings keep flying off the leader.
- **Static parents:** a wreck or installation sends its own position too, so its Defense Units patrol around it rather than around where they spawned.
- **Children of the other side:** attackers spawned by a Freighter or a GAALSIEN cruiser are its children too, so they hear its leash and patrol around it. Around a moving parent they fall behind, as above; they take no slot.
- **Spawn points:** manual Defense waves are placed around the target (`GVK-Universal-Waypoint-DefensesManualSpawn`), not the wreck, so a Unit can start near or outside its 3 km radius. One that never hears the signal acts as a wanderer (KPLs, no leash).
