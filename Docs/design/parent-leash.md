# Parent leash

## Note 46

Parent leash: Units stay near the grid that spawned them, and those Units never create KPLs (GVK-Settings#709; checked against MES `d85b7b3`). Generated by `tools/New-LeashGroup.ps1`.
- **Parent side:** `GVK-Universal-TriggerGroup-LeashBeacon` on every behavior that spawns Units (Defense waves, air support, Elite escorts, Convoy leads, Freighters). Every 5 s it sends `GVK_LeashGround` (3 km) and `GVK_LeashAir` (5 km) with `[MatchReceiverChild:true]`, so only the sender's own children hear it. Each signal carries the parent's position (`GVK-Universal-LeashBeacon-Waypoint-Self`).
- **No owner check:** `[MatchSenderReceiverOwners:false]`, because the owner check is on by default and a parent's children are not always its own faction (a DERELICT wreck spawns GAALSIEN Defense Units, a GAALSIEN wreck GAALSIEN ones, a Freighter its own Defenses and enemy attackers). The child filter alone works for every pairing.
- **Unit side:** every Unit carries `GVK-Unit-All-TriggerGroup-LeashGround` or `-LeashAir`. Hearing the signal sets `GVK_HasParent`, which blocks `CreateKPL`, and moves the Unit's patrol centre to the parent (`[UseCommandWaypointAsPatrolReference:true]`). After 15 s without it, `GVK_AwayFromHome` blocks `GVK-Unit-All-Condition-CanStartCombat` and the GVK-TriggerTags-SwitchToPatrol tag sends the Unit back to Patrol, around where it last heard its parent. The next signal clears it.
- **Letting go:** a Compromised parent sends `GVK_LeashRelease`. A Unit that stays away for 3 more minutes lets go by itself, which covers a parent that despawned or drove out of range. A released Unit patrols around its spawn point again (`[ClearCustomPatrolReference:true]`), fights anywhere and still creates no KPL.
- **Moving parents:** children of a Freighter, cruiser or Convoy lead follow it while they can hear it. Patrol picks up a new centre at the start of its next leg, so they trail a few seconds to a minute behind (GVK-Settings#723). A parent that outruns the 3 km/5 km signal leaves them at the last place they heard it.
- **Static parents:** a wreck or installation sends its own position too, so its Defense Units patrol around it rather than around where they spawned.
- **Children of the other side:** attackers spawned by a Freighter or a GAALSIEN cruiser are its children too, so they hear its leash and stay with it instead of being left behind.
- **Spawn points:** manual Defense waves are placed around the target (`GVK-Universal-Waypoint-DefensesManualSpawn`), not the wreck, so a Unit can start near or outside its 3 km radius. One that never hears the signal acts as a wanderer (KPLs, no leash).
